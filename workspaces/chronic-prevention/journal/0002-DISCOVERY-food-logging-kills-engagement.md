name: food-logging-kills-engagement
description: Food logging is the single highest friction health behavior — 80% abandonment by 90 days. Hawker meal frequency (1 number) is the viable proxy for nutrition input
type: DISCOVERY
---

**Finding:** Only 5% of Singapore desk workers sustain food logging consistently. Food logging has the highest dropout rate of any health tracking behavior (45% cite "too tedious" as reason for abandonment). This is not a UX problem — it's a fundamental behavior problem.

**Why non-obvious:** Nutrition apps (Noom, MyFitnessPal) treat food logging as the default input. For prevention-level users (not weight-loss motivated), the friction-to-benefit ratio is catastrophically bad. A pre-diabetic desk worker who eats 5 hawker meals a week is not going to log every rice dish.

**The viable alternative:** Hawker meal frequency (self-reported: "how many hawker meals did you eat this week?") is low friction, correlated with the metabolic risk input (high-GI carbohydrate exposure), and can trigger a specific behavioral nudge ("lower-GI hawker option to try"). One question, not 30 food entries.
