description: Pre-diabetes is 80%+ undetected and entirely asymptomatic — creating a uniquely actionable prevention window that no consumer product addresses
type: DISCOVERY
---

**Finding:** ~16–18% of 25–44 Singapore adults have pre-diabetes (HPB estimate), but <20% know it. That's 1 in 6 desk workers. The progression to diabetes is 5–10 years and reversible through lifestyle change — but no daily-engagement product sits in this window.

**Why non-obvious:** Most preventive health products target people who already feel something wrong (pain, burnout) or who are already diagnosed. The pre-diabetes blind spot is invisible — users don't seek a solution because they don't know they need one. The marketing challenge is entirely different from "I have back pain and want relief."

**Implication:** The product doesn't treat pre-diabetes — it makes the *accumulation of risk* visible before clinical diagnosis. The emotional hook is not "you are sick" but "you are on a trajectory, and this week you moved it."
