description: The product has no single metric to measure "daily value" in the prevention window — without this, retention hooks are diffuse
type: GAP

**Finding:** Unlike fitness apps (step count, calories) or weight loss apps (kg lost), metabolic disease prevention has no single proxy metric that updates daily and is meaningful to the user. HbA1c changes take 3 months to move. HRV trends take weeks to establish. Risk scores are HSA-constrained in language.

**The risk:** If the daily engagement hook ("open the app") has no clear immediate value proposition, the 30-day dropout cliff applies. The morning briefing recovery score + trend direction is the best available proxy, but it is not as viscerally motivating as "you hit your 10,000 steps."

**What this means for product design:** The weekly summary (1-page trend narrative) may be more important than the daily notification for retention. The daily hook must feel like a "check-in" not a "score." This is a hypothesis that needs a/b testing at MVP stage.
