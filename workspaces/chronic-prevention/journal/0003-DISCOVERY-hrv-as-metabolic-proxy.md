name: hrv-metabolic-proxy
description: HRV is simultaneously a stress marker and an insulin resistance proxy — wearable data provides metabolic monitoring without blood draws
type: DISCOVERY
---

**Finding:** Low HRV (RMSSD < 20ms) is associated with 2–3× higher Type 2 diabetes risk in cohort studies, mediated through autonomic nervous system dysregulation (which drives insulin resistance). This means a wearable device already provides a continuous, non-invasive proxy for the core physiological driver of pre-diabetes progression.

**Why non-obvious:** HRV is conventionally framed as a stress/recovery metric (Whoop, Oura). Its metabolic health implications are less widely discussed in consumer wellness contexts. The product can use HRV data for dual purpose — stress coaching AND metabolic trajectory monitoring — without requiring any additional hardware or blood tests.

**Constraint:** Population-level correlation does not equal individual prediction. HRV alone is insufficient for individual risk estimation — it must be combined with demographics, baseline labs, sleep, and activity in a multivariate model.
