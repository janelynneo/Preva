name: b2b2c-channel-not-customer
description: In B2B2C corporate wellness, the employer is a distribution channel, not the customer. The product must first succeed as B2C or no employer mandate will save it
type: DISCOVERY
---

**Finding:** The modal corporate wellness failure is: employer mandates app → employees download under pressure → use for 2 weeks → never open again. This is the failure pattern for >80% of corporate wellness programs. The product must deliver personal value within the first 3 uses before the employer framing becomes visible.

**Why this is non-obvious:** B2B2C looks like a shortcut to consumer acquisition. It is not. The employer is a trust anchor and distribution mechanism, not a substitute for consumer product-market fit.

**Design rule:** The onboarding experience must deliver a "this is actually for me" moment within 48 hours of first use — before the employee sees any employer branding or corporate framing. The employer channel is invisible in the first-use experience.
