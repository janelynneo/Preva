## Discovery: Metabo's Competitive Differentiation

### The Core Insight

Existing wearables (Apple Health, Google Fit, Oura, Whoop) are **data display platforms** — they show you numbers from your wearable. They don't tell you what those numbers **mean for your health trajectory**, and they don't give you **daily behavioral recommendations** based on your patterns.

Metabo is the **missing interpretation layer** between raw wearable data and meaningful behavior change.

### Why Wearables Fail at Behavior Change

#### Apple Health / Google Fit
- Show you **what happened** (steps, HRV, sleep hours)
- No interpretation, no recommendations, no coaching
- Data dump, not insight
- You have to figure out what to do with the numbers yourself

#### Oura Ring
- Better: tracks personal baseline (30-day rolling)
- Shows "readiness score" based on HRV + sleep + activity
- **BUT:** No chronic disease risk model, no metabolic context
- Recommendations are generic ("recovery day recommended")
- No behavioral coaching or daily engagement loop

#### Whoop
- **Strongest recovery/strain scoring** in market
- **BUT:** No coaching layer, data-rich but insight-poor
- "Strain" score means nothing without context of your job, stress, diet
- Subscription-only (~$239/year), no chronic disease focus

### Metabo's Moat: The Interpretation Layer

```
Wearable → RAW DATA → Apple Health / Google Fit → "You took 8,000 steps"
                            ↓
Wearable → PERSONALIZED → Metabo → "Tuesday is YOUR most active day.
                                         Your HRV is 12% above YOUR baseline.
                                         Your recovery is trending up.
                                         Keep it up — Tuesday workouts work for you."
```

### The Three Differentiators

#### 1. Metabolic Wellness Index (MWI)
- **What it is:** Tier 1/2/3 based on Cox PH survival model calibrated to Singapore demographics
- **Why competitors can't copy:** Requires clinical validation, Singapore-specific calibration, years of data
- **User value:** "Am I healthy?" answered in a way that's actionable, not scary

#### 2. Personal Baseline + Anomaly Detection
- **What it is:** Day 1-28 learning YOUR normal, Day 30+ flagging deviations
- **Why competitors can't copy:** Oura has personal baseline but no anomaly detection for metabolic risk
- **User value:** "Is what I'm doing today normal for ME?"

#### 3. Daily Engagement Loop (Morning Briefing + Evening Check-In)
- **What it is:** Consistent daily touchpoints that build habits
- **Why competitors can't copy:** Requires sustained UX investment, behavioral science
- **User value:** Transforms data into daily ritual
