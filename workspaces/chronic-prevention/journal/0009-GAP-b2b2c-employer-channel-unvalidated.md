**Finding:** Metabo's revenue model (S$5-12/employee/month B2B2C) assumes Singapore employers will buy standalone metabolic wellness benefits. No customer discovery interviews with Singapore HR decision-makers were conducted. The analysis does not establish: what budget line employers draw from (EAP vs. health insurance vs. discretionary wellness), who the economic buyer is, what triggers a purchase decision, or how the free-to-paid employer conversion funnel works.

Intellect (SG, Series A) is already in the corporate mental wellness B2B space, suggesting this market segment is being actively sold to. Metabo would compete for the same HR budget.

**For Discussion:**
1. Is the employer wellness benefit the actual channel, or is the employer benefit a user acquisition subsidy rather than the primary revenue model?
2. What is the minimum employer size where the S$5-12/employee/month economics work? (Small businesses <50 employees may not have dedicated wellness budgets)
3. Is B2C (S$12-18/month) a viable primary channel given Singapore consumer willingness to pay for health apps?
