## Demo Script (recommended 3–5 minutes)

### 1. Onboarding (30 sec)
"This is Metabo — it tracks your metabolic wellness using your wearable data. Let me show you how it works."

### 2. Morning Briefing (45 sec)
Show the recovery card, HRV trend arrow, sleep hours, and today's recommendation.

### 3. Evening Check-In (45 sec)
Complete all 5 questions: sleep quality, soreness, energy, stress, hawker meals.

### 4. Weekly Summary (45 sec)
Show the 4-metric trend display with arrows and weekly narrative.

### 5. MWI (1 min)
Show the tier result (Tier 2 🟡 Monitoring Recommended) and explain the two-layer ML model:
1. Cox PH survival analysis for 3–10 year risk
2. Anomaly detection for daily recommendations
